SECURITY AND PRIVACY

Your loads, your customers and your rates are yours.

Avanza is built so every company sees only its own data and every login stays under control.

Every company, isolated

Your data lives apart from every other company’s. The database rejects any query that crosses from one company to another, and automated tests check it across the whole system.

Passwords nobody knows

Stored with one-way encryption (scrypt): neither we nor your admin can see them. Each person sets their own from a link sent to their email.

Sessions under control

Sessions expire on their own, you can sign out of every device at once, and after 5 failed attempts the account waits before trying again.

Everyone sees what they need

Permissions by team and by screen. Rates and costs are only visible with permission: they’re filtered on the server, not just hidden on screen.

Everything on record

Load changes (phases, milestones, sign-offs) are logged with who and when. An issued invoice never changes: to fix it, you cancel and reissue.

Built on AWS

Avanza runs on Amazon Web Services. Files (PODs, invoices) are kept in private storage and opened through links that expire, and every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

Your data is never shared with anyone without your permission.Learn more about security →

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Coordinating loads is free forever, with unlimited users and no credit card. You only pay if you want invoicing and collections, or to connect your trucks and GPS.

Do I have to move my whole operation over?

No. Start with your next load and bring in the rest when you're ready.

Do I need GPS?

No. Every milestone can be entered by hand. If you have GPS, it's one more source of the same data.

Does it work if I never cross the border?

Yes. Avanza works the same for US-only, Mexico-only or cross-border operations.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.

Is my data kept apart from other companies?

Yes. Each company is its own isolated account; no outsider sees your loads, customers or rates.
